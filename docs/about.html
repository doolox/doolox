About Doolox

Uber For Websites

  1. Home

Doolox is the Uber for websites. Just as Uber removed the hassle of owning a car and dealing with maintenance, we remove the complexity of building, hosting, and maintaining a professional website. You simply subscribe monthly, and we take care of everything - design, development, hosting, updates, support, email, SEO, and more - so you can focus on your business instead of technical details.

Most people and small businesses struggle with websites. They either pay high one-time fees for a site that quickly becomes outdated, or they try to manage it themselves and get stuck with broken pages, security risks, and endless technical headaches. Doolox solves this by offering a complete, ongoing service. We don’t just build your site and disappear - we stay with you, continuously improving it and making sure it remains fast, secure, and effective.

Our process is designed to push you forward. We guide you through every step, from initial design choices to going live, and then keep the site updated with fresh content, security patches, and performance improvements. You never have to chase freelancers, renew hosting separately, or worry about hidden costs. Everything is included in one transparent monthly subscription.

Reliability is at the core of what we do. Your website runs on solid hosting with continuous uptime. Your email system is set up and managed properly. Search engine optimization is handled so your site stays visible. Support is always available when you need it. We treat your online presence the same way Uber treats every ride: dependable, simple, and stress-free.

In short, Doolox turns website ownership from a complicated, expensive, and time-consuming project into a smooth service. You get a professional site that works, stays current, and grows with you - without ever having to become a technical expert yourself.

Website building and maintenance through a monthly subscription - you get design, hosting, updates, support and everything you need. Simple, reliable and without hidden costs.
